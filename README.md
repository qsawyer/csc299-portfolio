# CSC 299 Portfolio

| Project         | Kind | Repository                                   | What it does |
|-----------------|------|----------------------------------------------|--------------|
| hockey-stats    | web  | https://github.com/qsawyer/hockey-stats      | A website where a player can enter stats from their team and their games and then have those stats tracked and displayed across all the games.          |
| hockey-shootout | game | https://github.com/qsawyer/hockey-shootout   | A game that runs in the terminal where you simulate a shootout in hockey, you choose where to shoot and the goalie chooses where to cover then you see if you scored or not. It is best out of 5 shots.          |
| lift-log        | CLI  | https://github.com/qsawyer/lift-log          | A tool in the command line meant for gym goers to replace their standard notebooks when it comes to logging all information related to their workouts. They can also see their highest personal PRs aswell as how often they have worked out that week and a few other statistics.          |
